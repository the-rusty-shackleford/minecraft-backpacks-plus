# D-0026 — Projectiles in a backpack count for the weapon in hand

Accepted 2026-09-24. Rusty: "I dont think ammo in your backpack is seen by weapons. My bow
shows as empty even though I have many arrows in my backpack. That should not be the case,
please correct that."

Vanilla's projectile lookup checks the hands, then the inventory, and stops. NeoForge fires
`LivingGetProjectileEvent` at the end of that lookup with what was found; a listener may
substitute a stack. Backpacks+ listens: when the game found nothing and the shooter is a player,
the first cell of the first bag holding something the weapon accepts is lent, the worn bag
first (D-0025's bag), then any bag in the inventory, hotbar first, and the offhand. What is in
the hands or the inventory still wins, as it does without a bag.

A bag's contents are an immutable component (D-0004), so the weapon cannot be handed the bag's
own stack. It is handed a copy marked under `custom_data` with the bag's address and the cell.
The one place a bow or crossbow takes its shot out of that stack is `ProjectileWeaponItem.
useAmmo`, and vanilla offers no hook there, so a mixin at its return does two things: drops the
marker from the ammo copy and from what was taken, so the arrow that flies (and the one loaded
into a crossbow) stacks with plain arrows; and on the server, when something tangible was taken,
removes that many from the bag's cell, storing the bag and marking its inventory changed. An
Infinity bow, creative, and the client's own prediction take nothing and debit nothing.

Rejected: moving a stack of arrows out of the bag into the inventory on the first draw (the
bag would empty itself into the player's pockets, the very clutter the bag exists to avoid);
a live view over the bag's cells (D-0004 forbids exposing stored mutable stacks); a quiver
mount (a mount holds one gear item, not a stack of consumables).

Ranged Weapons Mod's guns feed from magazines by their own rules and are not touched.

Verified by four GameTests: a bow with an empty inventory sees the worn bag's sixteen arrows,
marked; the shot leaves fifteen, the arrow entity flies unmarked, nothing leaks into the
inventory; spectral arrows in the inventory win over the bag; an Infinity bow works off the bag
and takes nothing; a crossbow loads from a bag carried in slot 7, the charged arrow unmarked,
the bag's cell one short and its neighbour untouched; a bag of stone and bread lends nothing,
and no bag lends nothing.
