# D-0018: Retire existing backpacks; replacement bags must be crafted

Accepted 2026-09-18 from Rusty's migration correction: "Just get rid of existing bags
entirely; all bags going forward will require crafting."

Do not map legacy tiers to Backpacks+ or grant replacement bags. Remove legacy bags
during the eventual approved pack transition. The earlier proposed leather/copper,
iron/gold and diamond/netherite mapping was not accepted and is not implemented.

Preserve and return stored items, including nested contents and upgrade-held items,
outside the removed bags. No instruction authorized deleting those contents. Stowed's
holstered stack still migrates to the independent Quick Slot, preserving its components.
Refuse or explicitly report any case that cannot be accounted for without loss or
duplication; preserve the source backup and never silently truncate excess items.

Read-only audits and local-copy rehearsals are authorized. Rusty specifically approved
copying saved player inventories and Sophisticated Backpacks storage to local session
scratch after automatic review initially rejected that export. Private world/player
data must stay outside the repository. Production changes and releases remain held.

The first saved-data audit found live holster stacks, a loaded nested container, and
external backpack records not accounted for by player inventory references. Player-only
conversion is therefore insufficient. The saved-region locator must account for other
containers, entities and placed bags before legacy removal is declared safe.
