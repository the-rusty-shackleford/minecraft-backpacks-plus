# D-0027 — The worn bag's cells sit on the player's own inventory screen

Accepted 2026-09-24. Rusty: "I think pressing E with a backpack equipped should also show you
backpack inventory alongside personal inventory and allow you to move blocks between them
freely." Their two calls, asked and answered: the bag's grid to the **left** of the vanilla
inventory screen, which keeps armor, the 2x2 grid, the recipe book and the Curios button; and
shift-click from the inventory goes **into the bag first**.

## How

Vanilla's inventory menu is one object per player, made when they join, on both sides; a bag
is put on and taken off during play. So the menu gains forty fixed slots (36 storage cells, 4
mounts) over a live container, `WornBag`, that resolves the worn bag on every call: cells beyond
the worn tier are inactive (vanilla neither draws nor clicks an inactive slot), and with no bag
worn all forty are. On the server a cell's writes go through the same `BagInventory` adapter
the bag's own menu uses, rebound whenever the bag, its identity or its revision changed under
it, so D-0004's immutable component storage is honoured and the bag's revision advances; on the
client the cells are a snapshot of the client's copy of the bag, refreshed on revision change,
and a click's prediction is overwritten by the server's slot sync. A stack the bag can no
longer take (the bag left the chest slot between click and commit) goes back to the inventory
or drops rather than vanishing.

The slots are added by a mixin at the tail of the menu's constructor, their first index read
off the menu at that moment: Quick Slot adds a slot of its own after ours in the pack, and
anything hard-coded would have landed on it (the first build's test did). Shift-click from an
inventory or hotbar slot tries the bag first (storage before mounts, by the tier's admission
rules), except for armor that would equip into an empty armor slot and an offhand item with the
offhand empty, which keep vanilla's rule; shift-click from a bag cell goes to the inventory as
vanilla does for any extra slot. The mounts across the panel's top take what a mount takes
(long or small gear) and refuse blocks.

The screen mixin draws the panel behind the cells when a bag is worn, sized to its tier, with
the bag's name over it, and moves the screen right when the panel would run off the left edge
(the recipe book, if open at such a width, may overlap it; a narrow edge case).

## Rejected

- Replacing the inventory screen with the backpack screen while a bag is worn (loses armor,
  crafting, the recipe book; Rusty chose the panel).
- Adding the slots only while a bag is worn (a menu's slot list is fixed for its life; adding
  and removing would break slot indices the client already holds).
- A client-only panel with custom packets for moves (vanilla's slot machinery, drag, number
  keys and shift-click would all need re-implementing).

Verified by two GameTests (the cells' activity following the bag on and off, a set and a take
reaching the bag's component; shift-click into the bag, a helmet still equipping, a pickaxe
into the bag, a mount refusing a block, shift-click back out, and nothing taken without a bag)
and a client capture of the screen.
