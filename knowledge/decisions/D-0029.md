# D-0029 — Picked-up items go into the backpack too, stacked the way the inventory stacks

Accepted 2026-09-27. Rusty: "when your main inventory is full, pickup-able items do not
automatically get picked up and slot into your inventory. They should do that, and if the item
is already in your backpack it should stack just like the regular inventory works in that
regard." Release authorized in the same message, once verified without regressions.

## The rule

The inventory and the bags are placed into as one inventory would be. The inventory's own add
tops up a matching stack before it starts a new one; with the bags that becomes four steps:

1. top up the inventory's stacks of the same thing (selected slot, offhand, then the rest, the
   inventory's own order);
2. top up the bags' stacks of the same thing;
3. the inventory's own add for what is left (its empty slots; creative's discard; any other
   mod's wrap of the call);
4. the bags' empty storage cells.

So a stack of cobblestone already in the bag is added to rather than a new inventory stack being
started, which is what "stack just like the regular inventory" asks; with no stack of that kind
anywhere, an empty inventory slot is used before a bag cell, as before. The bags are the worn one
and then any carried in the inventory (hotbar first) and the offhand, the order D-0026 finds ammo
in. Mount cells are never filled by a pickup. What the bags refuse (portable storage, D-0003) or
cannot hold stays on the ground as it would have. The same four steps run for an arrow pulled out
of the ground: arrows in the bag are where a bow finds them (D-0026).

The placement is the pure `domain.PickupPlan` (one pass over a bag's storage cells, top-up or
fill-empty); `BagPickup.add` runs the four steps. It is reached by a MixinExtras `@WrapOperation`
on the `Inventory.add` call in `ItemEntity.playerTouch` and in `AbstractArrow.tryPickup`: a wrap,
not a redirect, so another mod wrapping the same call still runs as step 3. On a client, with no
bag, or with an empty stack it is exactly the vanilla call.

A bag open on screen (a right-clicked `BackpackMenu`) is written through that screen's own
container: a write around it advances the bag's revision, which the screen checks, and the screen
would close under the player. The worn bag's cells on the inventory screen (D-0027) and beside a
chest (D-0030) rebind to the new revision on their next read.

## Rejected

- Only overflowing into the bag when the inventory is full, leaving the bag's matching stack
  alone while an inventory slot is empty: Rusty asked for the bag's stack to be added to "just
  like the regular inventory".
- Wrapping `Inventory.add` itself: `/give`, crafting results and every mod's insertion would start
  landing in bags.
- Filling mounts: they are holsters for gear chosen by the player.

## Verification

`PickupPlanTest` (5 JUnit, every partition of pass, count, cells and limit). `PickupGameTests`
(11 real-server tests through the real `playerTouch`: overflow onto the bag's stack then its first
empty cell, a named stack left alone, the bag's stack before an empty inventory slot, the
inventory's before the bag's, vanilla for kinds the bag lacks, a partial pickup, a full bag, no
bag, portable storage and a tool only a mount could hold, a carried bag open on screen staying
open, the inventory screen's panel showing the pickup, an arrow in the ground, an unpickable
arrow). Seven of them fail with the two mixins removed, the four regression cases pass either way.
`CuriosGameTests.aPickupOverflowsIntoTheCuriosBag` on the back slot, where players wear bags.
Seen live on the network fixture: a broken chest's iron ingots at the player's feet stacked onto
the worn bag's iron while the wheat, coal and planks went to the hotbar.
