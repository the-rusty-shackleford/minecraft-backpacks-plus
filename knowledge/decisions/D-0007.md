# D-0007: Dedicated gear mounts increase with crafted tiers

Rusty approved the proposed mount counts and long/small split on 2026-09-17:
"Those mounts sound good".

| Tier | Long tool or weapon mounts | Small-item mounts | Total mounts |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Basic | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Reinforced | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| Expedition | 2 | 2 | 4 |

These are dedicated gear slots, additional to the working ordinary storage capacities
of 9/18/36 in D-0006. Long gear mounts on the sides; small gear mounts on the outer face
or hangs from the pack, following D-0001. Exact item eligibility, stack limits, access
controls and Quick Slot exchange behavior remain for the design interview.

This decision approves the mount progression, not a storage schema or interface design.
Implementation still waits for completion of the interview and the resulting design review.
