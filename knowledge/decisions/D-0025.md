# D-0025: G is the bag's key alone, and the mount row stays beside the hotbar

Date: 2026-09-23. Status: Active. Amends D-0024 (the quick slot is no longer a G choice
even without a bag) and D-0011 (the row closes up before it lifts).

Rusty, the evening of 2026-09-23, with 0.2.3 on the box:

- "G should not apply to Quickslot, only backpack slots. No backpack, G does nothing."
- "it seems like my friends backpack slot bar, when the largest backpack is equipped with
  4 slots, is not being shown in the bottom right like always but is being shown in the
  compact way. That sucks. When you fix this, make sure it does not clash with the ammo
  indicator UI."

## G

G never offers the quick slot. Its choices are the worn bag's mounts, each occupied
mount's deposit, and the held-stack deposit; with no bag worn there is nothing to browse
and a press, a tap or a wheel with G down does nothing. H remains Quick Slot's own key.
D-0024's "the quick slot only when no mount is offered" is withdrawn: two keys, two mods,
no overlap. `GearChoices.build` takes no quick-slot flag and refuses zero mounts; the
`QUICK` kind, the quick-slot swap through G and its revision bookkeeping are gone. Quick
Slot's presence still matters to G in one way: G yields while its driving controls own the
player.

## The row

What the friend saw: the Expedition bag's four mounts at 24 pixels a cell, beside the
hotbar and its quick slot, overflow the screen by a few pixels at common GUI sizes (1280
wide at scale 3 is 427 GUI pixels; the four mounts need 428), and D-0011's fallback then
lifted the whole row above the status icons. Two or three mounts fit, so the bar had
"always" been at the bottom right for them until the largest bag.

The row now stays beside the hotbar wherever the mounts can fit there, closing up from
24 to 22 (frames touching) and then 20 (frames sharing a border, as the hotbar's own cells
do), and lifts above the status icons only when even that overflows (the 320-pixel
minimum, where nothing fits beside the hotbar). The mounts' places depend on the mounts
alone, so nothing moves when G goes down: the held-stack deposit cell goes at the end of
the row when it fits there at the row's pitch, else one row up above the quick slot's
column. The rule is `domain.GearLayout`, pure, pinned by JUnit at the friend's widths,
the minimum, both hands and every width from 320 to 1000.

The ammo indicator. Ranged Weapons Mod's counter sits in the bottom-right corner directly
above the hotbar's row and moves up while the gear bar is browsing in that row, because the
deposit cells and text rise into its space. It decided that by recomputing this mod's old
fit formula; that would have drifted the moment the pitch changed. Backpacks+ now says it
itself, `GearClient.browsingBottomRight()` (a gesture is open, right-handed, the row beside
the hotbar and not lifted), and the counter reads that. Idle, the row and the counter never
overlapped and still do not: the counter's box ends one pixel above the row. The lifted
held cell sits under the counter's raised position.

Rejected: two rows of two at the bottom right (D-0011's one horizontal row, and the second
row would sit exactly where the counter does); keeping the held cell in the row by
shrinking the mounts further while browsing (the mounts would jump on every press);
leaving the quick slot as G's choice when no bag is worn (Rusty: G does nothing then).
