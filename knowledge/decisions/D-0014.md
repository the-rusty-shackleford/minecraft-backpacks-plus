# D-0014: Restore the original backpack materials; retain crafting and dyes

Rusty on 2026-09-17: "lets revert back to the textures we had before all these
changes, but keep the receipes and dye mechanics."

This supersedes D-0012's replacement artwork and D-0013's simplified atlas. Restore
the exact original vanilla material references, face assignments and UVs: brown wool
canvas on Basic, green terracotta on Reinforced/Expedition, brown terracotta leather,
dark oak straps, iron fittings and black terracotta lining. Preserve all geometry,
animation pivots, size and mount placement. Remove the generated atlas from the mod.

Keep all approved recipes, safe component-preserving upgrades and sixteen dye colors.
Undyed bags render the original materials without tint. Only explicitly dyed bags
use the neutral counterpart of their canvas material, tinted from DYED_COLOR; leather,
fittings and lining retain their original textures. The full item and animated pieces
use the same selection. No new mixin or gameplay behavior is introduced.

The generated concept and texture experiments are superseded, not current targets.
Release remains HELD for Curios, lighting, migration and the remaining validation.
