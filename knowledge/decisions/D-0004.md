# D-0004: Rustic survival equipment with visible tier progression

Accepted from Rusty's design-interview answer and two visual references on 2026-09-17.

Backpacks should feel rustic and suited to survival. Avoid an ornate RPG-fantasy or fairy
aesthetic. They should visibly progress into better backpacks through crafting/upgrading.
Higher tiers may be physically larger, but size increases are not required to communicate
progression. Keep the previously approved modest proportions as the starting reference.

The supplied backpack1.png and backpack2.png illustrate the desired mood, not a model to
reproduce. Rusty explicitly said not to overfit to them. Use the references to understand
practical construction, straps, handles, pockets and grounded materials; do not copy their
exact silhouette, accessory layout, ornamental details or textures into the mod.

Working art direction for later visual review: earthy cloth/leather surfaces, functional
stitching and straps, restrained metal fittings, and better reinforcement/construction as
tiers improve. Exact materials, palette and model details are not individually approved
by this interpretation. Build original artwork and review it on the player in-game.

Tier count, upgrade ingredients, capacities and whether mount count grows with progression
remain open. The references do not decide those gameplay questions. Continue the design
interview one question at a time, waiting for an answer before replacing the question UI.
