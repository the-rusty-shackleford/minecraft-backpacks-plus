# D-0006: Working storage capacities of 9, 18 and 36 slots

Rusty proposed "maybe 9, 18, 36" during the design interview on 2026-09-17, replacing
the suggested 18/36/54 progression.

Use the following working capacities for the reviewed prototype:

| Tier | Ordinary storage slots | Rows of nine |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Basic | 9 | 1 |
| Reinforced | 18 | 2 |
| Expedition | 36 | 4 |

Dedicated gear mounts are separate from these ordinary storage capacities, as in the
question Rusty answered. Their count, eligibility and access behavior remain undecided.
Treat the numbers as a working balance choice to evaluate in-game, not an instruction
to skip the remaining interview or design review.

Existing legacy bags can contain more items than these capacities. Migration must preserve
that overflow through an explicitly designed conversion path; never truncate it to fit.
