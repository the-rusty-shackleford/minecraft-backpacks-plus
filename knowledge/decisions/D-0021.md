# D-0021: Elytra hides the worn bag and its mounts

Accepted 2026-09-18 after a real-client capture showed the wings intersecting the bag.
Rusty selected hiding the worn backpack and mounted gear whenever elytra is equipped.
This applies while standing as well as flying. The separate Quick Slot already follows
its own unconditional elytra hiding rule.

This is a client presentation rule. The bag remains equipped, keeps every item, stays
accessible through B and the G gear bar, and continues to contribute dynamic light.
Its hidden draw/open gestures do not claim the player's arms or suppress held items.
A separately hand-carried bag retains its top-strap carry and opening animation.
Removing elytra restores the worn model and mounts immediately. Curios' own visibility
state remains authoritative and is never rewritten by this cosmetic rule.

Use the same worn-visibility predicate for the bag layer, worn gestures and Quick Slot
compatibility. Do not gate storage, synchronization or Luminance on it. This decision
authorizes the visual correction; release remains HELD.
