# D-0011: Keep the gear bar horizontal at every supported GUI size

Date: 2026-09-17. Status: accepted correction from Rusty.

Rusty pointed out that the first in-game capture showed a vertical bar, contrary to the
chosen horizontal design. The browser prototype already kept the mounts in a horizontal
row when compact; the first game implementation incorrectly made a column.

Mount slots must always share one horizontal row. When there is enough room, that row
continues outward from Quick Slot beside the hotbar. When the side margin cannot hold
the row, align its outer end with Quick Slot and lift the entire row above status icons.
Mirror this arrangement with the main arm. Keep the original Quick Slot at its established
location and retain existing H/G behavior. Do not introduce a vertical fallback.

Use NeoForge's actual reserved health/armor/food/air heights for compact clearance;
inspect small and wide real-client views. This corrects presentation only and does not
change inventory, payloads, capacities, animation scope or the release hold.
