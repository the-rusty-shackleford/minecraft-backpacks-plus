# D-0035: A bundle rides in a bag

Date: 2026-09-29. Status: accepted. Supersedes D-0010 for bundles only.

Rusty, 2026-09-28: "I am unable to place bundles in my backpack for some reason. Probably an
anti-recursive safeguard, which is good, but should not apply to bundles. No infinitely nested
backpacks, but you can carry bundles in there".

D-0010 rejected bundles with the other portable inventories, by the `forbidden_containers` tag
(`minecraft:bundle`) and by refusing any item with bundle contents. Its reason was D-0010's own:
no infinite storage. A bundle does not break that. A bag fits in no container item
(`BackpackItem.canFitInsideContainerItems` is false), so no bag goes into a bundle and none nests
through one, and a bundle's own room is bounded by the game.

- A bundle, empty or filled, is admitted to a storage cell like any other item.
- What it carries must be what a bag could hold itself: a bundle holding a chest, a shulker box
  or a bag's refused item is refused, and so is a bundle holding such a bundle (`storable` looks
  inside, recursively).
- Chests, barrels, shulker boxes, other bags and inventories retained in items stay refused, as
  D-0003 and D-0010 have them.

Verified by `aBundleRidesInABagButCarriesNoStorageAndNoBagInto`: an empty bundle and one of
apples and string admitted; one carrying a chest refused, and one carrying that bundle; a bag goes
into no bundle. It failed with the old rule. The pickup test's example of storage that stays on
the ground is now a shulker box.
