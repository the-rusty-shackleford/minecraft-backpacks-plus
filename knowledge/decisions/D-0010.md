# D-0010: Allow utility blocks; prevent nested storage expansion

Date: 2026-09-17. Status: accepted, explicit clarification from Rusty.

"I don't mind backpacks holding furnaces and stuff like that. Really, my issue at the
core of it is I don't want backpacks turning into an infinite storage hack."

Ordinary furnaces, smokers, blast furnaces, hoppers, dispensers, droppers and similar
utility blocks are permitted. Having an inventory when placed is not by itself a reason
to reject an item. This settles the utility-block boundary left open by D-0003.

Retain D-0003's explicit prohibition on backpacks, chests and similar storage containers.
Reject portable nested inventories, including empty backpacks, bundles and shulker boxes.
A utility-block item that actually retains another inventory is also rejected. Bags may
still be put into ordinary chests. Use actual item data/capabilities and extensible tags,
not names containing strings like "bag" or constructing arbitrary mod block entities.

Minecraft 1.21.1 gives ordinary furnace items an EMPTY CONTAINER component by default.
Tests must distinguish that harmless default from retained contents. Modded portable
containers with private storage formats require pack-specific tags/compatibility checks;
do not claim arbitrary future mods are detectable without their APIs or identifiers.
