# D-0012: Rustic material progression, staged crafting and canvas dyes

Accepted by Rusty on 2026-09-17 after the concrete texture and recipe preview:
"that looks good". This authorizes implementation; release remains held.

Keep the existing compact shapes, scale, pivots and mount fit. Replace provisional
vanilla block materials with original canvas, leather, stitches and dull hardware.
Basic is plainly sewn canvas/leather; Reinforced has stronger seams and reinforced
edges; Expedition has waxed canvas and more durable leather/hardware. Keep the
survival aesthetic. Wax is a crafting/art material, not a waterproofing mechanic.

Normal 3x3 crafting, with optional dye in the bottom-center cell:

| Result | Pattern | Materials, excluding optional dye |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | `SLS / LWL / LDL` | 5 leather, 2 string, 1 wool |
| Reinforced | `ILI / SBS / IDI` | Basic bag, 4 iron ingots, 1 leather, 2 string |
| Expedition | `HLH / IBI / CDC` | Reinforced bag, 2 iron blocks, 2 honeycomb, 1 lead, 2 leather |

Symbols are local to each row. In Expedition H is honeycomb, L lead, I iron block,
B the prior bag, C leather and D dye. Leave D empty for the undyed recipe.
Any wool color is accepted in Basic. All sixteen vanilla dyes work in all tiers.
Canvas takes the dye; leather, thread, lining and metal retain their natural materials.
One bag plus one dye also permits shapeless recoloring at its existing tier, including
in the inventory 2x2 grid. One dye replaces the old color; no mixing in this version.

Upgrading consumes the prior bag and preserves its contents, mounted items, UUID,
custom name and other item data. The existing dye persists unless a dye is supplied.
Undyed bags use each tier's natural palette. Remap mount cells by category and order
because ordinary storage expands before the mount cells. Additional cells start empty.
Malformed counts or overflow refuse the craft and retain the input, never truncate it.

Use standard crafting menus and recipe books with custom serializers for safe result
assembly. Use Minecraft's DYED_COLOR component and client-only canvas tinting. No mixin.
The imagegen material concept in devtools/crafting-preview is a direction reference;
actual in-game captures are the acceptance evidence for the implemented textures.
