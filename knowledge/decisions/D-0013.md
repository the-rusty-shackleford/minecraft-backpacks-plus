# D-0013: Quiet pixel materials instead of dense texture detail

Rusty's in-game review on 2026-09-17: "the backpack texture lookks very busy.,
I liked the pixel direction more". This supersedes the dense weave/stitch execution
of D-0012; its recipes, dyes and survival material progression remain approved.

Use broad, restrained pixel clusters and quiet canvas. Simplify stitches and highlights;
no checkerboard weave, diagonal texture striping or tiny all-over leather grain. Tier
progression comes from the existing supports/pockets, leather reinforcement, simple
hardware and material palette. Keep the approved size, geometry and mount placement.
Normalize the atlas to 64x64, or 16 logical pixels per material tile. Rusty explicitly
approved deterministic resizing after the image generator ignored requested dimensions.

Review the result on the actual player, with natural and dyed canvas. The prior generated
concept and first dense atlas are historical references, not the current visual target.
Release remains held.
