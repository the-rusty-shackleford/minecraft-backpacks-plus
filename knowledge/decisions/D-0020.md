# D-0020: Independent Quick Slot uses hip or pocket verse with a visible bag

Accepted 2026-09-18. Rusty chose "Small items at hip; large items hidden" for the separate
Quick Slot while Backpacks+ is visibly worn. The backpack's own 2/3/4 mount slots remain
independent and keep their approved models and placements. No additional visual mount
or storage slot is added to the bag.

A guarded client adapter supplies the existing tracked equipment snapshot's visibility
to Quick Slot. Curios' render toggle and normal unequip restore Quick Slot's ordinary
body placement. Held/inventory-only bags do not trigger it. Quick Slot storage, swaps
and dynamic light continue whether or not its body item is visible. Bowl meals and
elytra retain their unconditional hiding policy.

The companion D-0011 records the body fit and resource override details. This is an
approved cosmetic integration, not release authorization.
