# D-0016: Larger torches and lanterns on backpack mounts

Rusty requested larger mounted torches and lanterns, closer to their placed-block scale.
Keep the existing torch/lantern items and resource-pack models; increase their mounting
scale rather than replacing them with unrelated art or changing light brightness.

The original small-item limit of 0.26 blocks measured the entire generated sprite quad,
including transparent pixels. In Minecraft's 16px art, the torch occupies 10px vertically;
the lantern item occupies 13px, compared with 11px for its placed model including handle.
Match those visible heights: 10/16 block torches and 11/16 block lanterns. Three-dimensional
resource models are measured and fitted to those maximum lengths. Other mounted items
retain the approved limits and orientations.

Mount lamps upright. Correct the known vanilla sprite padding, separate adjacent mounts,
and seat the back surface against the outer pocket using the measured model depth. Verify
normal/soul variants, a pair of lanterns, side contact and held-bag ground clearance in the
real third-person client with shaders. Resource packs with unusual custom sprite padding
may still need an item-specific placement adjustment in the future.

This is a cosmetic change; light emission remains entirely controlled by D-0015's backend.
Original backpack materials, recipes, dyes and gameplay remain. Release stays HELD.
