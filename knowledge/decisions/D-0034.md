# D-0034 — A packed vehicle is portable storage and never goes into a bag

Accepted 2026-09-28, found by the audit for the Carried change (D-0032) and fixed in it on Rusty's
"yes" to fixing what the audit found.

A vehicle picked up with the wrench is one `vanillawheels:vehicle` item carrying its chest in
Vanilla Wheels' own `vanillawheels:cargo` component (a list of container contents), not in the
vanilla container component the nesting check reads. `BagContents.storable` passed it, so a truck
with a full chest could be put in a backpack, picked up into one (D-0029) or given into one:
nested portable storage, which D-0003 forbids. Reproduced on the gametest server with the real
Vanilla Wheels 1.9.4 and Metals and Materials 1.0.3 jars loaded (`-PtestMountMods`): the check
said a packed truck holding 64 diamonds and 64 gold blocks was storable.

The item joins `#backpacksplus:forbidden_containers` as an optional entry, the way Sophisticated
Backpacks' bags are listed: data, and harmless without Vanilla Wheels. The whole item is refused,
empty or not, as chests and barrels are: an empty packed vehicle is still a vehicle whose chest
the player can fill again once it is out. Trailblazer, Trailer and Farmer's Truck register no
packed item of their own (their vehicles are Vanilla Wheels' item).

Rejected: reading the cargo component in `storable` (Backpacks+ would name another mod's
component in code, and an empty truck would pass); a Vanilla Wheels item capability (a change in
another mod for a rule that is this one's).

Verified by `VehicleGameTests.aPackedVehicleNeverGoesIntoABag`, registered when Vanilla Wheels is
loaded: it failed before the tag entry and passes with it; a give of the truck to a player with a
full inventory leaves it out of the bag.
