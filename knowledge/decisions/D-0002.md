# D-0002: Dedicated mounts and a focused backpack feature set

Rusty's 2026-09-17 answers select dedicated backpack mount slots for several items,
integrated with Quick Slot. Mounted gear is not automatically chosen from ordinary
backpack contents.

Required first-version capabilities: compatibility with Curios' equipment slots,
storage tiers, and opening a backpack by right-clicking while holding it. Rusty said
the other Sophisticated Backpacks utility features are not needed. Backpack nesting
was explained but no behavior was chosen for the replacement.

Rusty then explicitly requested an in-depth design interview covering function, looks,
interface and related choices before backpack implementation. The MDK foundation may
remain, but no storage, mounting or UI design is approved by these answers alone.
The interview and reviewed design are the next gate.
