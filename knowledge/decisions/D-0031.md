# D-0031 — The worn bag's panel on the creative inventory

Accepted 2026-09-28. Rusty: "when in creative mode, the backpack inventory in the E menu overlays
on top of the regular inventory instead of working normally. Please make it work normally", with
a release to follow.

## Why it broke

In creative, E opens `CreativeModeInventoryScreen`. Its inventory tab wraps every slot of the
player's `InventoryMenu` in a `SlotWrapper` at a position it chooses by index: armor, offhand,
the 27 rows and the hotbar; every index past vanilla's 46 falls into its default branch and lands
on the hotbar row (y 112). D-0027's forty cells sat over the player's own slots there, drawn
without their panel. And the creative screen is client-authoritative: it edits the player's
inventory menu itself and sends each changed slot as `ServerboundSetCreativeModeSlotPacket`, which
vanilla's handler takes only for slots 1 to 45, so an edit of a cell would have been dropped and
the client left showing a stack the server never had (Quick Slot switches its own appended cell
off in creative for that reason).

## How

- **Layout, client.** `InventoryPanel.creative(width, worn, inventoryTab)`: shown only on the
  inventory tab, with a bag worn, from 379 GUI pixels wide (panel, gap, the 195-pixel screen and a
  2-pixel margin each side); never beside, since there is no recipe book. While shown, the screen
  is laid out as for a screen the panel's width wider (`AbstractContainerScreenMixin`, at the end
  of the container screen's own layout, for the creative screen only, because the creative screen
  places its page buttons and search box from `leftPos` straight after it), which stands the
  window half a panel right and centers the pair. `CreativeModeInventoryScreenMixin` stands the
  wrapped cells where `WornBagSlots.place` puts the menu's own (the tab wraps in menu order, so a
  cell's wrapper has its index), draws `BagPanelArt` to the left, and lays the screen out again
  when a tab switch changes the widening (the tabs switch without a layout; the other tabs stay
  centered) or a bag goes on or off. EMI excludes the panel's column there too.
- **Edits, server.** `ServerGamePacketListenerImplMixin` hands a creative slot packet for one of
  the bag's cells to `CreativeBagCells.apply` once it is on the server thread: set when the player
  is in creative, the stack enabled and within its stack size, the cell active and admitting it
  (`WornBag.canPlaceItem`: no nested storage, mounts by size); otherwise nothing changes and the
  menu is sent back to the client. Every other slot goes to vanilla as before. No new payload and
  no slot layout change, so the registrar stays "4".
- **Clicks between cells, every screen.** A click on the panel's background with an item on the
  cursor counted as outside the screen and threw the item, on the inventory and chest screens
  since D-0027/D-0030 too; `hasClickedOutside` now answers false inside the panel's rectangle on
  all three (`BagPanelArt.inside`). NeoForge already treats a click on any slot as inside.
