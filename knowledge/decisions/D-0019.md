# D-0019: Discard legacy backpack contents instead of recovering them

Accepted 2026-09-18. Supersedes D-0018's content-recovery requirement.

After being told that the existing bag owner's ordinary inventory is full, Rusty was
asked where recovered items should go: labelled vanilla recovery chests at spawn or
having players empty their bags before release. Their answer was "Just get rid of em
its fine". The assistant confirmed removal of legacy bags and their contents, with
no recovery chests. No replacement Backpacks+ bags are granted; new bags must be crafted.

Stowed holster stacks remain separate: move those into Quick Slot without losing their
item components or overwriting existing Quick Slot contents. The existing read-only
audit copy remains local for rollback/evidence and must not enter a repository.

This authorizes the content-discard policy for the prepared transition. It is not a
release go or permission to mutate the production world before the held release.
