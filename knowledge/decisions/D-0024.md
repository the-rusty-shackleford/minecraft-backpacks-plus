# D-0024: Releasing G commits the highlighted cell, wheel or no wheel

Date: 2026-09-23. Status: Active. Supersedes D-0023's "Release G commits the
highlighted action only after scrolling"; D-0008's direct release-G exchange and
D-0023's explicit deposits remain in force.

Rusty, on 0.2.1: "When you hold G and let go, you expect it to swap with the
highlighted item but it doesn't. You have to scroll one click for that to happen.
It should work like I said is expected." The gear bar showed a highlighted cell the
moment G went down, and the release ignored it unless the wheel had moved (the
`moved` flag), a guard D-0023 wrote in against tapping G by accident.

The accepted interaction is:

- Releasing G commits the highlighted choice, whether or not the wheel moved. A
  press and release between two client ticks counts as a tap and commits too.
- The highlight opens on the last swap made (quick slot or mount) when it is still
  offered, else the first mount, else the quick slot. A deposit ("Put ... in bag")
  is never the default and is never remembered: it must be scrolled to, so a tap
  never puts anything in the bag. That is the safety the old guard gave, kept where
  it matters.
- Everything else is unchanged: a refused cell shows its reason and moves nothing;
  focus loss, a menu, a bag, hotbar or held-stack change cancels the gesture, and a
  key held through a cancel opens nothing until released.

The rule is a pure state machine, `domain.GearGesture` (press, tap, commit, cancel,
reset, the wheel opening early), and `GearChoices.defaultIndex`, both pinned by JUnit;
`GearClient` reads the step and acts. Protocol 4 is unchanged: the client sends the
same intents, only when.

Rejected: keeping the scroll guard and adding a "tap repeats the last swap" special
case (two rules for one release); defaulting to the quick slot when Quick Slot is
loaded (G is the bag's key; H is the quick slot's).
