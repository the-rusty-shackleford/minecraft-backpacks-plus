# D-0036: The client's view of the bag's cells follows the menu's sync, never a sync of the bag

Date: 2026-10-06. Status: accepted. Fixes D-0031's creative path (0.5.1 to 0.7.0).

## What happened

Bobandy_ on the box, 2026-10-06: the backpack "had all its contents dumped out and replaced with
spider eyes". The player's saves bracket it. At 23:02:23 UTC (`.dat_old`, five seconds into
creative) the Reinforced bag held 18 stacks: 7 and 7 spider eyes, three potions, 16 dynamite, two Silk Touch
diamond axes, 9 maps, two pumpkin pies, two shields, two gliders, shears, a Lure III fishing rod in
a long mount and a torch in the small one, at revision 4264. At 23:07:23 (`.dat`) it held 64 and 62
spider eyes in cells 8 and 17 and the fishing rod, at revision 4390. Bobandy_ was in creative from
23:02:18 to 23:05:31. No command touched the bag; nothing of it lay on the ground or in the other
player's inventory. 126 writes in three minutes is far more than a hand makes, and the spider eyes
went from 14 to 126.

## Why

The creative screen is client-authoritative. While it is open, the client's inventory menu carries
vanilla's `CreativeInventoryListener`, and after every click on the inventory tab (and when the
screen is laid out: opened, resized, or our own `rebuildWidgets` on a widening change) the menu's
`broadcastChanges` sends the server, as a creative slot packet, every slot whose stack the client
sees changed since the last broadcast. `CreativeBagCells` (D-0031) takes those for the bag's cells.

On the client, `WornBag` read the cells from a snapshot of the client's copy of the worn bag and
took a new snapshot whenever that copy was a different object or carried a different revision. A
sync of the bag (Curios's, or the chest slot's) delivers a new object every time, so every sync
replaced the whole view. With a round trip longer than the gap between clicks, the sync answering
click N lands after click N+1 has been predicted on the client: the view goes back to the state
after N, the next click's broadcast sends that older view as the player's edit, and the server
undoes N+1. Each undo is a write, so a new sync follows, older again than the edits in flight. An
item placed and then undone is gone; an item picked up and then undone is doubled.

## Reproduced

`devtools/creative_race.py` on the network fixture with Curios: a Reinforced bag on the back with
eight kinds, one stack each; the eight stacks moved one cell along and back (32 clicks through the
creative screen's own mouse handling, `creativeBurst`); every kind counted on the server. Through a
proxy holding each direction 75 ms (`devtools/lag_proxy.py`, a 150 ms round trip; the record is
[creative-race.md](../../devtools/verification/creative-race.md)):

| clicks apart | 0.7.0 | revision | 0.7.1 | revision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 ticks | conserved | +32 | conserved | +32 |
| 3 ticks | five kinds gone, torches 32 to 96 | +95 | conserved | +32 |
| 2 ticks | conserved by chance | +296 | conserved | +32 |
| 1 tick | the whole bag gone | +165 | conserved | +32 |

On localhost with no added delay 0.7.0 conserved at every period: the race needs a real player's
ping, which is why the fixture's single clicks in D-0031's record never showed it.

## The rule

The client's view of the bag's cells is the menu's own, as every other slot's is: seeded from the
client's copy of a bag when a different bag (identity or tier) is worn, then kept by the menu's
slot sync, a client-side write being a prediction that sync overwrites. A sync of the bag never
replaces the view. The server is unchanged.

What remains is vanilla's own exposure, the same as the player's other inventory slots: a slot
update that crosses a click on the same cell, and the client dropping inventory slot updates while
the creative screen shows a tab other than the inventory.
