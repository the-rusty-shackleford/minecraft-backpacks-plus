# D-0005: Three crafted stages — Basic, Reinforced and Expedition

Accepted from Rusty's design-interview answer on 2026-09-17.

Use three distinct crafted stages named Basic, Reinforced and Expedition, in that order.
Do not reproduce the six-tier leather/copper/iron/gold/diamond/netherite ladder.

Each stage should express the rustic survival direction in D-0004 through its construction
and capabilities. Higher tiers need not become larger. Exact recipes, capacities, mount
counts and visual differences remain interview/design-review questions.

Legacy Sophisticated Backpacks tiers must be mapped separately during migration. Reducing
the number of tiers does not authorize deleting excess items or truncating inventories.
