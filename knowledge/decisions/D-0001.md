# D-0001: Build Backpacks+ separately and migrate away from Sophisticated Backpacks

Accepted from Rusty's explicit request on 2026-09-17: name Backpacks+, repository
`minecraft-backpacks-plus`, with weapons/tools mounted on the sides and smaller gear on
the outer face or hanging from the pack.

Backpack development moves out of Quick Slot into this repository. The approved 80% scale
is the initial proportion reference. Mounting on the new backpack supersedes the earlier
requirement to conceal gear between the player and Sophisticated Backpacks.

The first version's storage/upgrade scope and the inventory source for mounted items are
still open; this decision does not choose them. The existing mod stays in the live pack
until the replacement and conversion preserve its stored items and are verified.

The instruction to start building is not authorization to release Backpacks+ or remove
Sophisticated Backpacks immediately. Quick Slot's release was deferred pending unfinished
features; neither mod has been published by this work.
