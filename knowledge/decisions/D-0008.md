# D-0008: Persistent gear bar and independent backpack quick slots

On 2026-09-17, Rusty selected the gear-bar concept and requested that it always live at
the bottom beside the original Quick Slot. Holding G enables scrolling through that
group; the bar is not a temporary menu in the middle of the screen.

Rusty clarified that, while equipped, a backpack's mounts are effectively additional
quick slots. The player still has their one original Quick Slot without a backpack,
and it must remain distinct from the backpack-provided slots.

Rusty then chose: "Release G swaps directly with the highlighted slot (recommended)".
The question explicitly contrasted this with selecting a slot for H, and specified
that selecting a mount leaves the standalone Quick Slot untouched.

The resulting interaction contract is:

- The player's original Quick Slot is independent of the backpack and always available.
- The equipped backpack contributes its approved 2/3/4 dedicated mounts to the persistent
  gear bar. Ordinary backpack storage is not part of that bar.
- Holding G redirects scrolling from the normal hotbar to this gear group. Releasing
  commits a direct exchange between the highlighted slot and the selected hotbar slot.
- Drawing from a mount does not pass its stack through, or displace, the original Quick Slot.
- H retains its original standalone-Quick-Slot swap behavior, subject to the existing
  driving-controls priority. It does not follow a newly selected backpack mount.
- The mount inventory belongs to the bag. Unequipping removes those HUD slots while
  preserving their contents in the bag; the player's own Quick Slot remains available.

This supersedes the earlier study's proposal to funnel mounted gear into the original
Quick Slot. It does not approve new storage internals, an extra selected-hand inventory,
or true-hotbar-extension mixins: the chosen behavior is a swap with the selected hotbar slot.

The [persistent-bar study](../design/persistent-gear-bar.md) previews the updated direction.
Exact spacing, visual borders, compact-screen placement, and the fallback when the former
hand stack cannot fit the chosen mount are still proposals. Final binding conflicts and
Minecraft GUI scales must be checked in the real client. The design interview remains
in progress; no gameplay implementation or release is authorized by this decision alone.
