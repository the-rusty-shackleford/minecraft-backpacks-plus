# D-0030 — The worn bag's panel beside a chest

Accepted 2026-09-27. Rusty, while D-0029 was being built: "Also, interacting with a chest should
also show backpack inventory." Taken as part of the same instruction, verified and released with it.

## How

The chest's menu gets what D-0027 gave the inventory menu: forty fixed cells over a live
`WornBag`, added at the tail of `ChestMenu`'s constructor on both sides, the first index read off
the menu (27 + 36 for a single chest, 54 + 36 for a double), inactive without a bag or beyond the
worn tier. Every vanilla chest screen uses that menu: single, double, trapped, barrel, ender
chest. Another mod's subclass of `ChestMenu` is left as it is, since its own shift-click may count
on the slots it knows; the cells are added only when the menu's class is `ChestMenu` itself.

Shift-click is taken over while the cells are there. Vanilla's sends a chest stack into every slot
after the chest's, from the last one back, which would have filled the bag's mounts first. Now:

- a chest stack goes into the player's inventory as vanilla sends it, and only what the
  inventory cannot take goes into the bag, storage before mounts (D-0027's rule for the bag);
- an inventory stack goes into the chest, as before (not into the bag: at a chest the player is
  putting things away);
- a bag cell goes into the chest.

The screen: the panel stands to the left of the chest screen with its bottom level with the
screen's, so it sits beside the player's inventory rows whatever the chest's height, and the pair
is centred as one, the chest screen half a panel right of vanilla, as D-0028 does for the inventory.
`InventoryPanel.of(width, worn, false)` decides (no recipe book here): under 360 GUI pixels wide the
panel is not shown and the cells are inactive, the screen vanilla's. Vanilla's `ContainerScreen`
draws its texture at the centred position rather than at `leftPos`, so both of its texture draws
are moved to `leftPos`; without that the chest's slots would sit half a panel off their wells. The
panel's drawing is shared with the inventory screen (`client.BagPanelArt`), and EMI keeps off the
panel's column here too. Putting a bag on, off or swapping tiers with the chest open lays the
screen out again.

## Rejected

- Shift-click from the inventory into the bag first, as on the E screen (D-0027): at a chest the
  inventory's stack is on its way into the chest.
- Adding the panel to every container screen (furnaces, hoppers, shulker boxes, modded screens):
  Rusty named chests; each other screen has its own layout and shift-click rules.

## Verification

`ChestGameTests` (7 real-server tests through the real `ChestMenu` and its clicks): forty cells after
chest and inventory, active to the worn tier, none active without a bag, another mod's subclass
untouched; chest to inventory with room, chest to bag storage with the inventory full and never a
mount, inventory to chest, bag cell to chest, a click carrying a chest stack into a bag cell, no bag
and a full inventory left vanilla. Photographed on the network fixture (single and double chest,
Expedition and Basic bags, 640x360, 427x240, 320x240, and with no bag;
[record](../../devtools/verification/chest-panel.md)), and shift-clicked there through the live
client's click path. The client mixin failed to inject on the first live launch, which the
gametest server (no client mixins) could not have caught: a local-variable hook on the texture
position scanned no target; `@ModifyArg` on the two texture draws with the full method descriptor
replaced it.
