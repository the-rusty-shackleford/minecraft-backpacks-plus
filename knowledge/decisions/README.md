# Decisions

- [D-0001](D-0001.md): Separate Backpacks+ mod with visible gear mounts and a staged migration.
- [D-0002](D-0002.md): Dedicated mounts, focused storage/equipment scope and an in-depth design interview.
- [D-0003](D-0003.md): Reject nested storage containers and carry the held bag by its top strap.
- [D-0004](D-0004.md): Rustic survival styling and visible tier progression; references are inspiration only.
- [D-0005](D-0005.md): Three crafted stages named Basic, Reinforced and Expedition.
- [D-0006](D-0006.md): Working ordinary storage capacities of 9, 18 and 36 slots, excluding mounts.
- [D-0007](D-0007.md): Two, three and four dedicated mounts by tier, split between long gear and small items.
- [D-0008](D-0008.md): Persistent gear bar; backpack mounts act as independent quick slots with direct release-G swaps.
- [D-0009](D-0009.md): Implementation authorized; early real-game third-person animation review and Fresh Animations compatibility.
- [D-0010](D-0010.md): Permit utility blocks while preventing nested portable storage.
