Backpacks+ / Crafting and color guide

Built for a longer journey.

The original Minecraft pixel materials are back. Craft and upgrade the same bag, with optional canvas dyes. See the actual bags and colors →

Craft the first. Upgrade the same bag.

Every recipe fits a normal 3 × 3 crafting table. Higher tiers consume the previous bag and preserve its contents, mounted gear, name and color. A dye in the bottom-center cell replaces the canvas color.

Red canvas; natural leather and hardware.

Basic

9 storage · 2 mounts
5 leather · 2 string · 1 wool
+ 1 red dye

Early survival supplies: leather straps, a wool lining and cord stitching. Any wool color is accepted.

Reinforced

18 storage · 3 mounts
1 Basic backpack · 4 iron ingots
1 leather · 2 string + 1 red dye

Add iron hardware, leather reinforcement and stronger stitching. Your Basic backpack becomes the upgraded bag.

Expedition

36 storage · 4 mounts
1 Reinforced backpack · 2 iron blocks
2 leather · 2 honeycomb · 1 lead
+ 1 red dye

A substantial iron investment plus beeswax for the canvas and cord for the field rigging. All materials are obtainable in the Overworld.

Sixteen dyes, natural materials

Every vanilla dye works on every tier. Color applies to canvas panels only; leather, straps and metal keep their original materials. The selector shows recipe ingredients; the in-game preview shows the actual colors.

Leave the dye cell empty for an undyed Basic bag, or to preserve the previous bag’s color when upgrading. An undyed bag uses each tier’s natural palette.

Recolor without rebuilding

One backpack + one dye, anywhere in the crafting grid, recolors an existing bag. No dye mixing in the first version. Items, gear mounts and custom names stay intact.

Upgrades move mount contents into the new tier’s dedicated mounts. New storage cells and extra mounts start empty; no existing item changes into an ordinary storage item by accident.

Progression: early leather gathering → ironworking → a larger iron investment and beekeeping. Wax is a crafting/art material here, with no new waterproofing mechanic.
